the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group (EDRDG) at Monash
University.

  KANJIDIC2:  http://ftp.edrdg.org/pub/Nihongo/kanjidic2.xml.gz
  Project home: https://www.edrdg.org/

jmdict-index.json.gz and kanjidic-index.json.gz in this directory are derived
works: a lossy, trimmed re-encoding of the above source files (produced by
scripts/build-jmdict-index.mjs and scripts/build-kanjidic-index.mjs), kept
under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 licence as the originals. They are not a
replacement for or authoritative copy of JMdict/KANJIDIC2 — consult the
source files above for complete data.

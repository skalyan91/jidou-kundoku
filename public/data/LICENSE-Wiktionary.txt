Wiktionary (the Japanese-language entries of the English-language edition,
en.wiktionary.org), via kaikki.org's structured Wiktextract extraction of it.

historical-kana-index.json is a derived work: built by
scripts/build-historical-kana-index.mjs, which scans the source JSONL for
entries carrying Wiktionary's own explicit 歴史的仮名遣い (historical kana)
annotation (a `forms` entry tagged both "hiragana" and "historical") and
extracts only the bare kanji-spelling + modern-reading -> historical-reading
kana triples those annotations state (keyed by each entry's own kanji
headword/redirects/alternative-spelling forms and its own modern reading,
not by the resulting kana reading alone) — none of Wiktionary's definitions,
glosses, example sentences, or other prose content are included. Kept under
the same CC BY-SA licence as the source. Not a replacement for or
authoritative copy of Wiktionary — consult the source above for complete
data, and see en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Wiktionary:Copyrights for full
attribution terms.
